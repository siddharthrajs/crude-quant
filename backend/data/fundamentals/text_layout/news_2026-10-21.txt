Stable domestic retail prices
    The export controls are intended to help ensure adequate

domestic supplies and support the government's fuel price
ceiling, introduced on March 13 to limit increases in domestic
gasoline, diesel and kerosene prices, MOTIR said.

    Maximum prices are calculated using average domestic
supply costs, changes in Mean of Platts Singapore assessments
and applicable taxes. The government will compensate refiners
for losses incurred under the program after it ends and will lift
the price ceiling and export restrictions once international oil
markets stabilize, MOTIR added.

    Together with fuel tax reductions, the measures helped
bring average retail diesel prices down to 1,843 won/liter
($1.28/liter) in the second week of October, down 8.1%
from 2,006 won/liter five months earlier, according to
MOTIR. Gasoline prices fell 7.7% to 1,857 won/liter over the
same period.

Iran's Araghchi says negotiations with US ongoing

Crude, product exports remain below pre-war levels

    Iran has opened "special routes" for Iraqi ships through the
Strait of Hormuz to help Iraq mitigate the cost of the ongoing
crisis in the waterway, Iranian foreign minister Abbas Araghchi
said in an Oct. 20 interview with Iraqi state news.

    In March, Iraq said the country was in talks with Iran to allow
some tankers carrying Iraqi crude to pass out of the Persian Gulf
through Hormuz.

    Iraq's state oil marketer SOMO and the oil ministry declined to
comment on the matter Oct. 21.

    "Tehran is aware of the extent of the losses and economic
burdens that Iraq has incurred as a result of the developments
in the strait," Araghchi said. "The Iranian side has made efforts to
mitigate these costs ... [including] opening special routes for Iraqi
ships to pass through the Strait of Hormuz."

    The comments come as a tanker was "struck by an unknown
projectile" 30 nautical miles off the coast of Iraq's port city Al Faw
on Oct. 20, according to the UK Maritime Trade Operations.

    UKMTO said there was no reported damage or environmental
impact and advised vessels to transit with caution.

    So far in October, Iraq's crude exports have averaged 3.4
million barrels/day through the Strait of Hormuz, according to
comments from Iraqi oil minister Bassem Mohammed Khudair
carried by state news INA Oct. 20.
